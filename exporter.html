    Activity

    What you get

    • Every chat as a readable Markdown file and as raw JSON, including archived chats and chats in projects.
    • Edited and regenerated replies, kept under "Other versions" at the end of each chat.
    • The images and files from your chats, saved next to the chat they belong to.
    • Your memory, custom instructions and projects.
    • An INDEX.md to browse everything, plus up to 20 bundle files that combine all chats for search or upload.

    Project files, code interpreter outputs and very old images may no longer be available from ChatGPT.

    Your privacy

    • Your chats go straight from ChatGPT to the folder you choose. Nothing passes through any other server.
    • It connects only to chatgpt.com and to the file links ChatGPT provides for your own files. Your login is never sent to those links.
    • No analytics, no tracking, no remote code.
    • This browser keeps only the export progress and a reference to your folder. Removing the extension deletes both.

    Works with ChatGPT Support for other major AI chat apps is coming soon.

    It uses the same requests as the ChatGPT website, so a ChatGPT update can break it until a new version is released. Not affiliated with OpenAI. ChatGPT is a trademark of OpenAI.
